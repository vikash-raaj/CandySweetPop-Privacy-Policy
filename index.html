Privacy Policy – CandySweetPop

Last updated: June 3, 2026

CandySweetPop ("we", "our", or "us") is a free mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you play.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.).

Game progress (levels, scores, lives) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

CandySweetPop uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and use data including device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. For details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use AdMob

Children's Privacy

This game is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. AdMob can be configured for child-directed treatment; if you believe your child has provided personal data, contact us immediately.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Google AdMob. We do not share data with any other third parties.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
